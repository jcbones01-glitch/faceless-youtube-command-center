## Editorial safeguards

- Keep global IEA figures separate from U.S. Berkeley Lab figures. Do not average them.
- A forecast/scenario interval is not automatically a statistical confidence interval.
- Distinguish a large electricity-consuming site's connection from generator interconnection.
- Do not substitute generator queue statistics for large-load connection evidence.
- Separate MW (power) from MWh/TWh (energy).
- Avoid universal “energy per AI prompt” claims.
- The previously supplied DOE $1.9 billion announcement remains excluded because it was not verified in the launch packet.
- Any real connection timeline used later must come from a specifically sourced public example, not a made-up generic number.

## Outstanding source task

Read the full Berkeley Lab connection report, not only its abstract, and determine whether a public utility/case example is strong enough and simple enough to use in the final narration.
