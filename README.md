# Faceless YouTube Command Center

Evidence-led source of truth for building an original, low-cost faceless YouTube documentary/explainer channel.

## Channel promise

**Explain familiar parts of modern life by revealing the hidden physical, technological, and economic systems underneath them.**

Primary territory includes:
- AI infrastructure and data centers
- power grids and electrical equipment
- semiconductors and chip fabrication
- undersea cables, cloud infrastructure, and telecom
- logistics and supply chains
- payment systems
- GPS and timing infrastructure
- industrial and scientific systems

This is not a generic AI-news channel, an “interesting facts” content farm, or a finance-advice channel.

## Editorial rules

- No claim that a “viral formula” has been scientifically established.
- Do not fabricate quotes, data, timelines, incidents, or competitor analytics.
- Separate projections from measured historical values.
- Distinguish association from causation.
- Use original explanation and visual structure; do not copy competitors' scripts, thumbnails, or branding.
- Track image/video/music rights individually.
- Treat current public view counts as snapshots, not reconstructed day-7 performance.
- Preserve unsuccessful experiments and ambiguous results.
