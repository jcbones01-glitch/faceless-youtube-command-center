# VID-001 Shot List / Visual Asset Plan V1

**Video:** Why Data Centers Can't Just Plug Into the Grid  
**Script:** scripts/VID-001_SCRIPT_V1_CLEAN.md  
**Planning runtime:** ~9–10 minutes  
**Timing note:** all timestamps are provisional until actual narration is recorded.

## Visual language

Use one recurring simplified grid diagram as the visual spine:

Generation → transmission → substation/transformers → distribution/direct service → data-center site

Add constraints to the same diagram as the story develops instead of introducing a completely new graphic for every concept.

Preferred hierarchy:
1. original diagrams/animation;
2. original charts redrawn from cited data;
3. licensed or generated contextual B-roll;
4. external screenshots only when necessary and rights/attribution are verified.

Do not use dramatic outage/fire footage unless the narration is actually discussing that documented event.

## B-roll policy

B-roll is optional support, not the explanatory core.

Good uses:
- exterior data-center campus;
- transmission towers;
- substations;
- transformer transport/manufacturing;
- server racks;
- construction cranes/electrical work.

Avoid:
- generic “hacker” imagery;
- fake control-room emergencies;
- random power-plant smoke stacks presented as the cause;
- unrelated disaster footage;
- stock video implying it depicts the illustrative project.

## On-screen source labels

For quantitative graphics, use compact labels such as:
- Source: IEA, Key Questions on Energy and AI (Apr. 2026)
- Source: Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (Jun. 2026)
- Example: Portland General Electric large-load process, accessed Sep. 2026
- Example: American Transmission Company load-interconnection guide

Full URLs belong in description/source notes rather than cluttering the frame.
