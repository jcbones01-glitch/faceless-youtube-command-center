## Minimal viable rough cut

The first rough cut can be assembled with:
- approved narration;
- Thumbnail-A-derived opening visual;
- D01–D09;
- simple motion, zooms and typography;
- no external stock footage;
- no music required.

This keeps the first pilot legally simpler and cheaper. Licensed/generated B-roll can be added later only when it materially improves comprehension or pacing.

## Visual repetition rule

Reusing D01 is intentional. Update its state rather than replacing it:
1. show path;
2. highlight interconnection;
3. add upgrade;
4. add capacity/operation/risk constraints;
5. return to completed path.

The recurring diagram gives viewers a stable mental model.

## Text-density rule

Narration explains detail. On-screen text should generally be:
- one label;
- one number;
- one question;
- or one short qualification.

Do not paste narration paragraphs onto screen.

## Final assembly gate

Before export:
- every quantitative graphic matches the source ledger;
- no rough AI concept board appears in the final edit;
- ATC and PGE remain named examples;
- D04 remains labeled as scenarios, not confidence interval;
- illustrative project and D07 remain marked illustrative;
- external assets, if added, are entered in the rights ledger.
