# VID-001 Creative Treatment

## Identity

**Video ID:** VID-001  
**Working title:** Why Data Centers Can't Just Plug Into the Grid

## Central question

Why can a company have a data-center plan and money for computers yet still face difficulty obtaining the electrical connection it needs?

## Story device

Follow one fictional project from site selection to a usable grid connection.

The project must be explicitly labeled as illustrative. Do not invent:
- a real company identity
- company quotes
- real project dates
- outage footage presented as though it depicts this project

Reuse one simple diagram throughout the video. Each section adds another constraint to the same picture.

## Viewer payoff

At the end, the viewer should know to ask three questions about a proposed data center:

1. Where will the electricity come from?
2. How will it physically reach the site?
3. How will the system handle the load and its changing demand?

## Draft opening

> Imagine you've bought the land, designed a data center, and lined up the computers. Now comes a question that sounds almost too ordinary to be a problem: where do you plug it in?
>
> A power line on the horizon doesn't answer it. A connection has to work for this site, at this scale, alongside everything already using the system.
>
> To understand what's getting in the way, we'll follow one imaginary data center through the machinery behind the cloud—and discover why building more power plants is only part of the answer.

Source basis: C05 and C08 in `research/VID-001_SOURCE_LEDGER.md`.
