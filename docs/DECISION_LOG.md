# Decision Log

## 2026-09-20 — Channel territory

**Decision:** Build a faceless mini-documentary/explainer channel about hidden systems behind modern life.

**Audience promise:** Explain familiar modern experiences by revealing the physical machinery, technology, coordination, and economic trade-offs underneath them.

**Reasoning:** This gives the channel a coherent audience identity while allowing topics across AI, engineering, computing, infrastructure, economics, science, logistics, and history.

**Rejected as primary positioning:**
- generic AI-news channel
- broad “interesting facts” channel
- finance-advice channel
- repetitive mass-produced faceless content

## 2026-09-20 — Evidence standard

**Decision:** Treat virality as an uncertain outcome and packaging/story choices as testable hypotheses.

Do not claim that research has established:
- a guaranteed viral formula
- one universally optimal video length
- one universally optimal editing cadence
- a universal CTR threshold
- that public competitor views reveal private retention/CTR/revenue

Preserve uncertainty and unsuccessful tests.

## 2026-09-20 — Pilot selection

**Decision:** Lead with AI infrastructure and electric-grid connections.

**Video ID:** VID-001

**Working title:** `Why Data Centers Can't Just Plug Into the Grid`

**Central question:** Why can a company have a data-center plan and money for computers yet still face difficulty obtaining the electrical connection it needs?

**Story device:** Follow a clearly labeled fictional/illustrative data-center project from site selection to a usable electrical connection.

## 2026-09-20 — Development budget

**Decision:** No incremental subscription spending is required during research/script development. Verify entitlements and an actual need before buying stock, voice, editing, API, or export credits.

## 2026-09-20 — GitHub governance

**Decision:** This repository is the durable project source of truth.

ChatGPT conversations may develop ideas, but important research, decisions, scripts, experiment records, and postmortems should be committed here.
